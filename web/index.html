Indonesian Token Tax

How many more tokens Indonesian text uses than English, across popular LLM tokenizers. Ratios are token multipliers, not prices.

This page runs entirely in your browser. Typed text is not sent anywhere. Tokenizer files are fetched from huggingface.co (and tiktoken ranks are bundled). No analytics.

Key findings

On FLORES-200 devtest, Indonesian has a word ratio of about 0.90 versus English, so the Indonesian side is not the longer text. The token ratio on that slice still runs about 1.25 (o200k_base) to 1.84 (Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3). Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Sailor2-8B share an identical vocab.json and produced identical counts here; their tokenizer.json hashes differ.

Tokenizers load on the first click (tokenizer files only, no model weights).

Method

Each pair is encoded as raw text with add_special_tokens=false (no chat template). The tax is sum(Indonesian tokens) / sum(English tokens). A bootstrap 95% CI resamples pairs 1000 times with seed 42. Leaderboard numbers are copied from the Python pipeline’s results/results.json.

Limitations

  • FLORES-200 is professionally translated, news-like, formal. Chat and slang are only the small synthetic slices.
  • Synthetic slices are small; their CIs are wide. Treat them as illustrations.
  • A ratio above 1 can come from the tokenizer or from Indonesian using more words than the English reference. Check the word-ratio column.
  • This page does not evaluate generation quality.
